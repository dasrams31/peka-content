# Gratisan AI: verified AI freebies in the bansos format

Status: ACTIVE (approved by user 2026-10-23, from the engagement analysis:
the best-performing Peka post ever is a universal freebie with low claim
friction; this pillar applies that formula to AI topics to bridge the
legacy freebie audience into the AI audience).

## Format
- 2x per week: Wednesday + Saturday, 14:00 WIB (cron peka-gratisan-ai).
- One offer per edition: an AI freebie that is genuinely free and
  claimable from Indonesia: free tool tiers with real value, free API
  credits, free AI courses, student/developer packs, free trials that
  do NOT need a credit card get priority.
- 3-slide ticket via the bansos ticket system
  (~/workspace/peka-content/bansos/bansos_design.py) with a per-edition
  design wrapper (pattern: bansos/oracle_deploy_design.py). Kicker for
  this pillar: "GRATISAN AI" (the locked "INFO BANSOS" kicker stays
  exclusive to real bansos editions).
- Blog post on blog.peka.ai.id in the bansos style (steps, terms,
  FAQ), links filled after posting.
- Platforms: Instagram + Threads + Facebook (info pillar, IG included;
  only SALES posts skip IG).

## Verification rules (non-negotiable)
- Offer must be verified on the OFFICIAL page during the run: what is
  free, how much, until when, card needed or not, region limits.
- If the user's own material conflicts with the public page, the
  user's material wins (precedent: Domain.CV edition, 2026-10-22).
- Never invent amounts, deadlines, or quotas. If a term is unclear,
  write it as unclear or pick another offer.
- Anti-repeat: check gratisan-ai/posted.log; the same offer may only
  repeat when its terms materially changed.

## Copy rules
Same as bansos: no em/en dash, hook line max ~60 chars, Threads text
max 500 chars with exactly 1 hashtag #AI, FB text max 1024 chars,
captions end with ONE interaction trigger (experience ask: "Ada yang
udah klaim?") plus a save trigger when there is a deadline.

## Evaluation
Part of the 2-week format test (2026-10-24 to 2026-11-06). Judged in
the Sunday engagement review against the HM news median: likes,
comments, saves/shares where the CLIs expose them. Flat after the test
window = rework or kill, per the user's standard.
